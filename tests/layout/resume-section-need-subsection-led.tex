% the subsection line, and the entry's unbreakable first block. That is one
% line more than the entry-led shape needs, and it is what sets the value.
%
% The filler count is not arbitrary. Measured on 2026-11-19, two passes each,
% with the page read by pdftotext: at 45 lines of filler "Target" and its
% subsection "Industry" end page one and the entry opens page two when the
% bound is 0, 4 (the value before #733), 5, or 6, and all three move to page
% two at every value from 7 to the committed 9. The runner's "no page ends with
% a section heading" check is what fails at the smaller values.
%
% Why the committed value is 9 and not 7 is not in this fixture: its first
% entry has one bullet. With two bullets the block is a line taller, and 7 and
% 8 still strand it at some page positions; #733 records that sweep.
%
% Re-derive the count if the filler, the fontsize, the margin, or a vertical
% token changes; the figure is a property of this exact content.
%
% Accepted page fill below the global floor (#733). Page one fills 87.3% of
% its goal and leaves 91.75pt blank, 6.75 lines of body leading: the room
% that was left when the heading arrived, short of the 7.15 lines the heading,
% its subsection, and the entry need together. A filler count that strands the
% heading at the old bound leaves at least the heading's own lines blank once
% the heading moves, so the short page is what this fixture tests rather than
% a defect in it. At
% the old bound of 4 the same page filled 93.9%, ending on the stranded
% subsection.
