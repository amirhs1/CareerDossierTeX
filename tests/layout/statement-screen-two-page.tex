\section*{Research Vision}

My research asks how computational results can remain understandable and
useful after the immediate project that produced them has ended. Scientific
software is written under deadline pressure, yet it is read and rerun for
years afterwards by people who were never part of the original team.

I approach that question from three directions: numerical methods whose
failure modes are legible rather than silent, tooling that records the
provenance of every published figure, and documentation written for the
reader who arrives without context.

\newpage

\section*{Current Directions}

My current work extends these methods to coupled nonlinear systems, where the
interaction between components is the part practitioners find hardest to
reason about. The goal is not a single model but a shared vocabulary for
describing where a model stops being trustworthy.
