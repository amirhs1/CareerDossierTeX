\subsubsection{\texttt{fontsize}}
\label{sec:fontsize}

\opt{fontsize} selects one whole-point type scale. The sizes it resolves to,
in points:

\toprule

\midrule
\endhead

Page furniture & 8 & 9 & 10 \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
